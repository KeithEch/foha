The Rot is a demented madhouse built upon cursed runes. It is filled with disfigured inmates, covered in fog, and concealing arcane secrets.

The warden of the Rot, an Archmage, has been using a powerful relic to conduct nefarious experiments upon the Rot's inmates.

The Rot of Madness sets up difficult moral choices and serves as a role-playing challenge for characters of good alignment.

It requires GMs to become familiar with all of its details and make any rule set adjustments before playing.
