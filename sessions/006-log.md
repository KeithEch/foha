# 006. Hero Section & Nav Bar Revisions — Log

**Date:** 2026-05-25
**Phase:** Phase 5 — Hero Section & Nav Bar Revisions

---

## What Was Done

Implemented design revisions to the hero section and nav bar across all breakpoints using Figma measurements and screenshot-based feedback. Added responsive art direction for hero background images, repositioned the hero logo and foreground character per Figma, wired the hero logo to CMS, redesigned the nav bar with a dark background and hide-on-scroll behavior, and applied a 1680px max-width constraint to both the hero and nav.

---

## Work Log

- **Hero background — responsive art direction:** Replaced single `<img>` with a `<picture>` element. `KeyBG_1920.jpg` (horizontal) serves at 1024px+; `Keyart_736.jpg` (vertical/portrait) serves at 768px and below. Both images copied from source assets. `.hero__bg` rule updated to also target `picture` for correct absolute positioning.
- **Hero foreground (`hero__fg`):** Hidden at 768px and below; shown at 1024px+ with absolute positioning. At 1440px, restored to original overflow positioning (`top: -73px; bottom: -65px`) using `width: auto; height: auto; aspect-ratio: 1539/1112`.
- **Hero logo positioning:** All breakpoints derived from Figma node measurements. 768px: centered horizontally at `top: 15px`, `width: 429px`. 1024px: `left: calc(50% + 209px)`, `width: 552px`. 1280px: `left: 75%; transform: translateX(-50%)`, `width: 580px` (centered in right half). 1440px: `left: 75%; transform: translateX(-50%)`, `width: 640px` (centered in right half, fluid).
- **Hero marquee heights:** 760px at 768px; 600px at 1024px+.
- **Hero CMS connection:** `logo_image` field already existed in `config.yml`. Added `data-content="hero.logo_image"` to `<img>` in `index.html`, wired `src` assignment in `load-content.js`, added default value `/images/rot-mad-logo.png` to `sections.json`. Hardcoded `src` acts as fallback if fetch fails.
- **Max-width 1680px:** Applied to `.nav` (with `margin-inline: auto`) and `.hero`. Nav background moved from `.nav` to `header` so the dark band remains edge-to-edge at any viewport width.
- **Nav bar redesign:** Background changed to Mud 800 (`--primitive-mud-800`) at all breakpoints. New logo `logo-navbar-v2.png` swapped in. Link color changed to Purple 900 (`--primitive-purple-900`). Height set to `--space-72` at 768px+. Logo image height `--space-40` at all breakpoints. Breakpoint-specific padding: `--space-32` at 768px, `--space-40` at 1024px, `--space-72` at 1280px+.
- **Nav hide-on-scroll:** Added `#nav-sentinel` div before `<header>` in HTML. Created `src/js/nav-scroll.js`: `IntersectionObserver` on the sentinel detects when user leaves/returns to the top; a passive scroll listener with `requestAnimationFrame` tracks direction mid-page. `header.nav--hidden` applies `transform: translateY(-100%)`. CSS `transition: transform 0.3s ease` on `header`; disabled via `prefers-reduced-motion` block.
- **Scroll-margin-top fix:** Was `--space-56` globally; updated to `--space-72` at 768px+ to match new nav height.
- **Top-sections padding:** `--space-0` at mobile (base), `--space-12` at 768px, `--space-32` at 1024px+. Added `--space-0: 0px` token to `tokens.css`.
- **Redundant nav background removed:** The 768px `.nav` block had `background-color` after it was moved to `header`; cleaned up.
